Job Title: Data Analyst

About the role:
We are looking for a Data Analyst to turn business data into clear insights for our product and operations teams.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Statistics, Computer Science or a related field
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis or business intelligence
- Strong skills in SQL, Python and Excel
- Experience building dashboards and reports in Power BI
- Solid understanding of statistics and data visualization
- Comfortable with data cleaning and preparing messy datasets
- Excellent communication skills

Nice to have:
- Tableau
- Experience with pandas, ETL pipelines and A/B testing
- Familiarity with Snowflake or BigQuery
