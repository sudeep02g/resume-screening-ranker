Job Title: Data Scientist

About the role:
Build and deploy machine learning models that improve our recommendation and forecasting systems.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics or a related field
- 3+ years of experience building machine learning models
- Strong Python, pandas and scikit-learn skills
- Good knowledge of statistics and feature engineering
- Experience writing SQL queries against large datasets
- Ability to explain results to non-technical stakeholders (communication)

Nice to have:
- Deep learning with TensorFlow or PyTorch
- Natural language processing
- XGBoost, Docker and AWS
